---
title: "How do I use AI?"
---

- I have one long-running AI chat that goes on for weeks, which I tune into every day, mostly with voice-to-text. It helps me straighten out my ideas and outline the structure: opener, three points, conclusion.
- I use a lot of individual prompts. Probably the most useful is: "Give me four to six bullet points to help me work on my opener."
- In a nutshell, I use a lot of AI. It's an incredible writing assistant: it lets me process far more information than I could by myself. **<u>However, the actual writing, my hands on the keyboard, is all me, once I have the bullets for each paragraph.</u>**
