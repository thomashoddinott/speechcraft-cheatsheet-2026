---
title: "How do I use AI?"
---

- I have one master AI session. I feed it my voice memos and bits of my speech, and it helps me get the structure in place: the opener, the three points and the conclusion, outlined.
- Then I use individual prompts for each step, for example:
  - "Give me four to six bullets for this paragraph."
  - Spotting repetition and fixing word order.
  - Building the card I memorise from.
  - …
- In a nutshell, I use a lot of AI. It's an incredible writing assistant: it lets me process far more information than I could by myself. **<u>However, the actual writing, my hands on the keyboard, is all me, once I have the bullets for each paragraph.</u>**
