---
title: "What if I completely freeze on stage?"
---

It happens. It's happened to me more than once: I've completely forgotten my lines, had to get my notes back out, and even restarted a speech. I'm sure it'll happen again. It's uncomfortable, and then, somehow, the world keeps turning. Every experienced member has bombed at some point, so trust me, they'll be on your side. It's part of the process, and the nerves go down quickly the more you get up and face them.
