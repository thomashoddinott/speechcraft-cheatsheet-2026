---
title: "What if I completely freeze on stage?"
---

It's happened to me more than once: I've completely forgotten my lines, had to get my notes back out, and even restarted a speech. I'm sure it'll happen again. It's uncomfortable... for a few seconds, then people move on. Every experienced member has bombed at some point, so trust me, they'll be on your side. It's part of the process, and the nerves go down quickly the more you get up and face them. Good things happen on the other side of fear 😉.
