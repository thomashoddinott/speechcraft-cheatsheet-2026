---
title: "How do I use AI?"
---

I have one long-running AI mega chat for my speeches, which I come back to when I'm writing, mostly interacting with voice-to-text in the beginning. It helps me straighten out my idea and outline the structure: opener, three points, conclusion.

I use a lot of individual prompts. Probably the most useful is: "Give me four to six bullet points to help me work on my opener/supporting point 1-2-3/conclusion". I use those bullets as a guide for my own writing.

AI is an incredible writing assistant: it lets me process far more information than I could by myself. **<u>However, the actual writing, my hands on the keyboard, is all me, once I have the bullets for each paragraph.</u>** I sometimes let AI choose a better word, or find inconsistencies in my writing, but even with that I am very careful because the skill of writing (and finding your own inconsistencies) is very valuable and I do not want to weaken it by leaning on AI too much.
