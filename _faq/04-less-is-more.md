---
title: "Less is more"
---

When I edit, I mostly cut. I'll lose almost half of a first draft, checking that every sentence carries its own idea and no two are doing the same job. Repeating the call to action is fine; repeating anything else is usually padding. If in doubt, go shorter: five minutes read aloud is about six on stage.

> Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away. — Antoine de Saint-Exupéry
