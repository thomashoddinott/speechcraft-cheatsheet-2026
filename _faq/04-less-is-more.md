---
title: "Less is more"
---

I'll cut almost half of a first draft, checking that every sentence carries its own idea and no two are doing the same job. Repeating the call to action throught a speech is fine (and is a common technique); repeating anything else is usually padding.

Sometimes I think something sounds cool in my head because I heard it on a podcast or youtube, but it doesn't quite fit. Focus on fit. Cut that cool saying or quote, even if it does indeed sound cool, because it's probably distracting from your message.

If in doubt with overall length, go shorter: five minutes read aloud at home is easily six on stage.
