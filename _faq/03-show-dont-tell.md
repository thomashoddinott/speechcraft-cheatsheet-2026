---
title: "Show, don't tell"
---

People don't like being told what to do even if it's 100% correct. You have to persuade them with ... storytelling! 

When I spot lots of "you should" and "you need to" in a draft (the second person), I know I've slipped into lecturing. I flip those lines into the first person: what I'm doing, or what I've done in my life. Stories, basically.
