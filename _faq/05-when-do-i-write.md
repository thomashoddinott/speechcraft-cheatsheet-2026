---
title: "When do I find the time?"
---

You have to make the time and then find a routine that works for you. Maybe you like writing a speech all in one day, if that works for you go for it. I like a more steady approach.

I try to do four a year speeches a year, so one every two to three months. I know I'm going to have to allocate some time to write most days when I'm cooking on my next speech. I've made a commitment to get good at this, so I'm willing to put the time it takes. You get out of Toastmasters what you put in.
